I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, co-advised by Prof. Hwee Tou Ng and Prof. Mike Zheng Shou. I completed my Master's at NUS, and obtained my Bachelor's degree from Wuhan University.

I am with Show Lab, NUS and ARC Lab, Tencent IEG on multimodal digital agent research. I previously interned at SenseTime Research Singapore and Tencent Map, Beijing.

I am interested in the interaction and productivity gains enabled by digital (computer-use/game) agents. Recently, I am focusing on game environments, as games offer rich visual observations, long-horizon goals, and dynamic mechanics. Improvements in perception, reasoning, and control in games will support the future embodiment of digital agents. Previously, I worked on low-level vision training on-device image restoration models for cameras. I was experienced with AI image signal processing (AI-ISP), image compression and restoration.
