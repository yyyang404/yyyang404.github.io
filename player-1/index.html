FROM CONTROLS TO DEMONSTRATIONS

Curated gameplay demonstrations provide the starting point.

02 / SFT DATA

Demonstrations for supervised initialization

Curated game-play data for aligned supervision.

The 6044 trajectories combines 5,360 gameplay with 684 in-game UI trajectories from 172 games. Recorded controls are mapped to the shared game control action space. Task instructions, reasoning, and keyboard-mouse action make each step a supervised learning example.

FROM DEMONSTRATIONS TO PLAY

Player-1-Gym enables learning by playing with verifiable rewards.

03 / PLAYER-1 GYM

Explore Player-1-Gym environments

Shared control demands with diverse game mechanics.

Each environment accepts keyboard and mouse actions and computes rewards directly from game state. Randomizing appearance, camera, scene, and control broadens the agent’s training experience.

FROM EXPERIENCE TO HELD-OUT ENVIRONMENTS

Experience gained through play transfers to held-out environments.

04 / BENCHMARK & RESULTS

Experience that transfers

MINECRAFT · MINEDOJO-300 · PLAYER-1-9B 38.33% task success after RL Player-1-9B: 38.33% task success after RL.

Player-1 learns through interaction in synthetic worlds, then transfers to games outside the RL training suite: From 10.54% after SFT to 38.33% after RL, a +27.79 percentage-point gain on the 300 tasks in MineDojo.

GameWAM, the strongest evaluated external baseline, reaches 12.63% under the paper's evaluation protocol.
